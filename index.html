Who Am I

About Me

I am a software developer, tester, and systems builder based in Leeds. Most of my work starts with a familiar problem: a business has outgrown spreadsheets, manual updates, disconnected tools, or a website that no longer reflects where the company is going.

I like building practical systems: websites that are easy to manage, mobile apps people can actually use, stock and sales tools, staff portals, dashboards, payment flows, and automations that remove repeated admin. I also test what I build, so the work is not just finished on a checklist; it is checked against the way people will use it.

Over the years I have delivered management systems, automation tools, mobile apps, and more than 100 websites for local and international organisations. I work best with people who need a technical partner who can understand the business, build the system, test the details, and explain the trade-offs in plain English.
